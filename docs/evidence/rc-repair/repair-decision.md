## RC-001 contract and boundaries

S is the committed control-generation transition. D is the durable one-use authorization stored on the canonical intent/protection after all provider account preflight. T is the invocation of broker HTTP transport. D shares the account serializer with S, conditionally renews the exact live worker/exit lease, and binds account, paper environment, canonical record/client identity, generation, executor, and exact transmitted payload hash. Internal lost-heartbeat state is checked during claim retries and synchronously after claim completion before transport.

S-before-D denies stale automated openings. Disable/re-enable cannot restore an obsolete generation. D-before-S remains potentially in flight when T is unobserved, even if the process dies or loses its lease. D is not proof of T; database leases do not fence a broker. No async operation is deliberately inserted between the adapter's final synchronous executor check and the transport invocation, but cross-process stop can still occur after D; that order remains tracked in the drain rather than being declared unsent. Claim-commit ambiguity does not authorize another POST. Identity reconciliation, not elapsed time, a 404, empty broker list, or lease expiry, establishes resolution.

Stop persists cancellation work for owned automated entries and reports disabled admissions, unresolved/draining identities, or a completed terminal drain. Owner manual orders are a distinct policy. Protection, reconciliation, quantity/price-reducing replacement and authorized closes remain available during entry disable. Protection and close writes carry their own exit lease. Emergency cancellation supersedes replacement. All broker POST/PATCH/DELETE calls remain outside retryable database callbacks.

Original account-seven GET barriers failed 0/4 on the unchanged candidate: emergency stop, ordinary disable, worker takeover, and lease expiry each allowed one forbidden POST.
