## Cash capacity

`orderLifecycle.synchronizeCash()` is exposed through authenticated owner `POST /api/robotrader/cash/synchronize`. It locks the same AccountCapacity document in a real Mongo transaction used by reservations and fill ingestion. It refuses synchronization while any application buy intent is nonterminal or reserved cash remains, or when broker open-order discovery reports a buy or is incomplete. The broker account read occurs inside that transaction and must identify the expected paper account, contain exact finite nonnegative cash, and complete within 15 seconds. Explicit stale state or an invalid/older-than-60-seconds provider timestamp is rejected. Timestamp-free account responses use the fresh authenticated account endpoint response as the evidence of current cash.

The absolute cash baseline becomes current broker cash plus the historical buy-spend counter. This preserves the existing cash-floor accounting while permitting confirmed sale proceeds and deposits to refresh capacity. It never increments capacity from locally assumed sale proceeds. Day/week/month SpendingBucket values and historical AccountCapacity.spentCents are unchanged. Repeated sync cannot double-credit cash. New reservations also obtain fresh account cash after locking capacity, ensuring stale cached account data does not authorize an entry and reflecting withdrawals conservatively.

The response separately names `brokerCashCents`, `reservedCents`, `historicalSpentCents`, and `cashSyncedAt`. Period spending remains a distinct constraint. This operation is deliberately conservative while entries are unresolved; it does not attempt to correlate partially settled broker cash with open fills.

## Coordinated close

`POST /api/robotrader/positions/:symbol/close` retains its existing route and accepts a stable `Idempotency-Key` header or `idempotencyKey` body. Optional `close.qty` limits the requested quantity; omitted quantity closes the freshly confirmed remaining position. Responses add a durable `close` record and retain lifecycle `intent/order` when an exit exists. `GET /api/robotrader/position-closes` exposes owner/account-scoped records.

PositionClose has unique logical-request and active-account/symbol indexes, included in trading readiness index initialization. Its orchestration shares the existing OrderProtectionLock with protection maintenance and ordinary manual sells. It identifies only stored application stop records, including recovery by durable client ID when acknowledgement lost its broker ID. Unrelated or unidentified exits block the close and are never canceled. Cancellation intent is persisted before DELETE; a fresh identity-matching broker lookup must show terminal status before a new exit can be submitted. Remaining whole-share position and available quantity are reread after cancellation; the shared lifecycle checks quantity again and retains the sole permission to POST.

The same close key resumes the same persisted operation across retries/restarts. The worker's existing reconciliation path resumes active closes independently of the browser. Protection creation and competing ordinary sells respect the active close reservation. New close work has a two-minute deadline. Before any sell exists, expiry stops further close submission and releases protection suppression. If a sell exists but remains nonterminal, recovery requests cancellation of only that close's linked broker order, once, then polls. Confirmed terminal cancellation releases protection suppression; uncertainty becomes visible `reconciliation_required` and blocks unsafe overlapping protection. Audit events and outbox records capture blocked/uncertain/deadline states.

Recovery relies on the configured scheduler continuing to run. A separate 30-second close-recovery timer runs independently of entry research and the normal five-minute reconciliation interval. Disabling entries or ordinary reconciliation does not disable this timer; explicitly disabling the entire scheduler does. It performs only a scoped Mongo existence read when idle. A persisted needsProtectionRecovery flag retains recovery work after a close ends until stop coverage is confirmed. Accepted-but-unacknowledged replacement stops recover through stable identity without a repeated POST. No software can guarantee a stop or cancel while its broker is unavailable; this implementation persists and surfaces that condition instead of claiming protection or issuing a competing oversized exit. External account activity outside this app is not controlled by the app lease, so unexpected exits fail closed based on broker discovery/availability.
